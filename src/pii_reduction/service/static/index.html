1. Pick a template

A template fixes what a caller may not choose — the source, the destination, and the switches that decide whether raw text survives. You choose from the menu it declares. This is what stops the service reading a table on your behalf that you could not read yourself.

2. Name the dataset and its row id

3. Configure the columns to reduce

Tick a column, then choose what to detect in it and how. Every option here is a quality choice: the worst it can do is produce a worse number, which the benchmark measures. Nothing here can change where data goes.

Trigger a run

A run is accepted immediately and executed on a single worker thread. The status below is metadata: row and field counts, timings, a config hash — never text.

Entity labels

What a configuration may name. Listing a label does not make a chain capable of detecting it — that is why the third column exists.
